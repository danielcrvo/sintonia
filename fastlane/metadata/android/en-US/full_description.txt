Sintonia is a simple, fast IPTV player for phones and Android TV. Add the playlist from your own provider and watch live channels, movies and series in one place.

IMPORTANT: Sintonia is only a player. The app does NOT provide, sell or include channels, movies, series or any other content. To use it you need a playlist (Xtream Codes, M3U link or M3U file) supplied by you or your provider, and you are responsible for only watching content you have the right to watch.

WHAT THE APP DOES
• Add playlists via Xtream Codes, M3U link or M3U file
• Live TV with a programme guide (EPG), channel numbers and progress of the current programme
• Movies and series with posters, details, seasons and episodes
• Continue watching where you left off, with automatic next episode
• Favorite channels
• Search across channels, movies and series
• Rename and hide categories
• Audio and subtitles, with a preferred language in settings
• Parental controls with a 4-digit PIN to lock categories
• Multiple playlists, choose the active one
• Light, dark or automatic theme
• English, Português (Brasil) and Español
• Works on phones and Android TV, with remote control support

PRIVACY
Everything is stored only on your device. No account, no login, no ads and no data collection. The playlist password and the PIN are kept in Android's secure storage.
